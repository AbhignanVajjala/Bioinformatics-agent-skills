# Contributor conduct

Treat contributors respectfully. Critique scientific claims and evidence rather than
people. Harassment, discriminatory behavior and publication of private information are
unacceptable. Maintainers may remove harmful content or restrict participation with a
recorded explanation. Contact maintainers privately through available repository contact
channels; do not expose affected people's personal details in public issues. An independent
appeal process is not yet established and is a governance task for the project owner.
