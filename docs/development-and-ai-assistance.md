# Development and AI Assistance

**Abhignan Vajjala initiated and maintains Bioinformatics Agent Skills.**
Development has used substantial AI-assisted coding, including OpenAI Codex,
for implementation, documentation and automated verification. AI tools are
acknowledged as development tools, not listed as human authors or independent
methodological reviewers.

Project-level maintainer attribution does not assert that Abhignan Vajjala
personally wrote every line, independently implemented every method, or has
already reviewed every scientific assumption. Existing Git authors, commits and
contributor credit remain unchanged. Display names alone are insufficient to
infer distinct human identities or the origin of individual lines.

| Responsibility or evidence | What must be recorded |
|---|---|
| Scientific requirements | Experimental unit, estimand, assumptions, scope and acceptance criteria; who proposed or approved changes |
| Implementation decisions | Method/software selection, parameters, trade-offs and actual contribution, including material AI assistance |
| Software verification | Commands, versions, inputs, hashes, outputs, failures/skips and CI records |
| Methodological review | Actual human reviewer, scope, execution performed, findings, independence and unresolved issues |
| Release responsibility | Maintainer decisions about merging and communicating scope; automated checks do not supply human scientific approval |

Executable tests and CI results are linked in [scientific evidence](scientific-evidence.md).
Independent biological validation and human methodological review remain
incomplete. AI-assisted implementation and passing automated tests do not by
themselves establish sound scientific reasoning, clinical applicability or
improved agent effectiveness.

The [technical walkthrough](maintainer-technical-walkthrough.md) supports future
human examination. Its review fields remain NOT_REVIEWED until an actual human
records the work. A role pass by the same model is not independent peer review.
Contributors should follow the full repository's
[contribution policy](https://github.com/AbhignanVajjala/Bioinformatics-agent-skills/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md).
Citation credit in [CITATION.cff](../CITATION.cff) names the project initiator and
maintainer and retains the existing generic contributors credit. It makes no
sole-code-authorship claim.
